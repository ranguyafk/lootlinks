Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle. When you downgrade, the change will take effect at the end of your current billing period.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, and PayPal. For Enterprise plans, we also offer invoice billing with NET 30 terms.
Is there a long-term contract?
No! All plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. You can cancel at any time and won't be charged for the next month. Enterprise plans may have custom contract terms.
Do you offer refunds?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied for any reason within the first 30 days, just contact support and we'll issue a full refund.
What happens if I exceed my limits?
All our plans include unlimited link creation and views. The main differences are in features and platform fees, not usage limits. So you'll never be charged extra for creating more links or getting more views.
