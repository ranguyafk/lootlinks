LootLinks allows you to create gated links that require visitors to view sponsor content before accessing your destination URL. You simply create a link, set the number of required sponsor views (1-5), and share it. When visitors click your link, they'll view the required sponsor content before being redirected to your destination.
Earnings depend on the number of views your links receive and the sponsor content displayed. Each completed sponsor view generates revenue. The more traffic you drive to your links, the more you can earn. Track your earnings in real-time through the creator dashboard.
We offer instant payouts with no minimum threshold. Once you've earned revenue from sponsor views, you can request a payout at any time. We support multiple payment methods including PayPal, bank transfer, and crypto payments.
