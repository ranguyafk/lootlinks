Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LootLinks

How does LootLinks work?
LootLinks allows you to create gated links that require visitors to view sponsor content before accessing your destination URL. You simply create a link in your dashboard, choose how many sponsor views are required (1-5), and share the link with your audience. When someone clicks your link, they'll view the sponsor content, and you earn revenue for each view.
How much can I earn?
Earnings vary based on your traffic and engagement. On average, creators earn $0.01 per sponsor view. With enough traffic, this can add up quickly! Our top creators earn over $1,000 per month.
Is there a free plan?
Yes! Our free plan includes unlimited links and basic analytics. You can upgrade to Pro at any time to access advanced features like custom domains and lower platform fees.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal. For Enterprise plans, we also offer invoice billing.
How do I get paid?
Payments are processed monthly via PayPal or bank transfer. You need a minimum balance of $10 to request a payout. Payments are typically processed within 3-5 business days.
Can I use LootLinks on multiple websites?
Absolutely! You can create and share LootLinks anywhere - your website, blog, social media, email newsletters, and more. There are no restrictions on where you can use your links.
What kind of analytics do you provide?
Our dashboard provides detailed analytics including total views, conversion rates, earnings per link, geographic data, and more. Pro users get access to advanced analytics with custom date ranges and export capabilities.
Is there a limit on how many links I can create?
No! All plans include unlimited link creation. Create as many gated links as you need for your content.
